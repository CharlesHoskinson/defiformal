# Prohibitions are the sole obstruction

**Status: PROVED**

`Adm = (R ∩ W) ∩ H` is not union-closed, hence not a sublattice of [[R02-lattice]].
Prohibitions are the only reason.

But only **one** of the 20 recorded prohibition rows (X2) is an enforceable
positive element set. See [[R06-trace-reduction]] for the consequence.
