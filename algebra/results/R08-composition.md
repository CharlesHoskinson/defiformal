# Composition on canonical forms is linear

**Status: PROVED**

`ex(A ⊕ B) = max_⪯(ex(A) ∪ ex(B))`, in time linear in the generator sizes.

**This is the compositionality result**: the canonical form of a composite is
computable from the canonical forms of the parts, with no reference to the rest
of the vocabulary. Holds on the definite fragment only.

Follows from [[R05-convex]]. The price of leaving the fragment is exactly the
loss of this theorem — see [[R04-prohibitions-cost]].
