# Clause polarity

**Status: PROVED**

Requirements and warrants are clauses with exactly one negative literal, hence
**dual-Horn**. Prohibitions are purely negative, hence **Horn**.

Consequence: models of requirements and warrants are closed under **union**;
models of prohibitions under **intersection**; their conjunction under neither.

Supports [[R02-lattice]], [[R03-no-moore]], [[R07-obstruction]].
Cited by: Schaefer dichotomy, Post's lattice, Pol-Inv Galois connection.
