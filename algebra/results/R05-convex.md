# The definite fragment is a convex geometry

**Status: PROVED**

A union-stable closure is anti-exchange **iff** its specialization digraph is
acyclic. Our digraph has 15 arcs, 58 SCCs, none non-trivial.

So `(E, Cn)` is a convex geometry **on all 2^58 subsets** — specifically the
down-set geometry of a 58-element poset.

Citations: Caspard & Monjardet 2004 §3 (singleton premise ⟹ union-stable, iff);
Edelman & Jamison 1985 Thm 3.2 (downset alignment iff union-closed);
Edelman 1980 Thm 3.3 (meet-distributive ⟺ convex geometry).
