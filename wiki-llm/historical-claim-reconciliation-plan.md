# Historical claim reconciliation author draft

The [OpenSpec author draft](../openspec/changes/historical-claim-reconciliation/proposal.md)
proposes four capabilities,18 requirements,45 scenarios and27 unchecked tasks.
It has not passed the nonauthor GPT-6/native Fable planning gate and contains no
implementation or newly verified mathematical result.

It starts from the delivered [18 claim-site findings](../review/semantic-kernel/claim-reconciliation/preparation-r2/REPORT.md)
and the [actual historical-instance audit](../review/semantic-kernel/historical-instance-audit/REPORT.md).
The former binds63 excerpts from22 files; the latter records the unchanged m5
run at4d42600 with its real nonempty counters. These records retain their original
identities; this draft does not rerun them.

The proposed work has three independently reviewable deliverables: source-specific
claim dispositions and selected prose corrections; a universal Lean bridge for
the exact LSTAR and PARSED_NEW unary graphs; and a wrapper that evaluates actual
bounded counters rather than trusting exit0. Final acceptance joins the evidence
without promoting measurements into proofs. All historical theorem statements,
proofs, generators, corpus inputs and prior reports remain unchanged.

The Lean bridge uses a literal58-symbol encoding and an independently defined
finite saturation executor. It proves equality to reachability for every input,
checks concrete antisymmetry, then instantiates the existing intrinsic extreme-point
and composition results. Fixed-source extraction and reviewed JavaScript algorithm
translation remain explicit trust boundaries; no verified V8/parser claim follows.
A full-instance statement outside the encoded unary fragment remains open if its
exact closure cannot be bound.

The correction policy preserves valid stronger arguments. A fully admissible pair
whose union contains a purely negative forbidden set has no admissible common
upper bound. Loss of union closure alone does not prove that result, and a
repairable mixed clause is insufficient. Wide CNF clauses likewise provide only
syntactic evidence unless a full-instance majority counterexample or proof exists.
The default bounded correction withdraws unsupported inferences without pretending
to refute stronger conclusions that might have another proof.

S9 operational sequential composition is accepted. S10 interfaces, M3 finite
participants and M4 operational regrouping remain separate pending packages.
Corpus adjudication owns source/identity judgments; all12 proposed evaluation
cases remain development-exposed and untouched evaluation stays blocked pending
its own freeze and selection. Full paper rewriting, complexity, deployments and
runtime adapters are outside this change.

The draft's author also authored historical audit and corpus research evidence;
an independent planning reviewer must not count this author as a nonauthor.
The current policy requires GPT-6 stock-harness implementation, same-candidate
nonauthor GPT-6 plus native Fable5.1 medium planning review, and native Grok plus
Fable result review. No Foreman or native review was invoked for this draft.
