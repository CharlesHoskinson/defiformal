The operational packages must preserve each preceding executor's actual histories, failures and state observations. A tree shape change keeps the same schedule and transaction boundary. Adding a participant needs a more selective observer; under atomic execution it also needs proof that the new participant cannot introduce an abort. Final balances alone do not establish these results.

The claims package intentionally starts with unconditional rational principal. It does not close indexed obligations, messages, finality, legal conditions or cross-domain settlement. Checked arithmetic does not establish correspondence with an optimized contract implementation. The remaining financial libraries, source-bound fidelity, serialized certificates, runtime adapters and untouched evaluation require further packages.

All 75 researched corpus units remain development cases. Research cards and advisory source reviews are not accepted deployment identities. The twelve original evaluation candidates are exposed; replacements must be selected after the kernel and evaluation policy are frozen.
