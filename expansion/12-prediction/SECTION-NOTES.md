## Lane narrative

**What the section introduction should say.** The category prices and settles a claim on a *fact* rather than on a price, and it is the residual of the sampling frame — three prediction venues plus two capital-allocation businesses the lane could not file elsewhere. It has three exclusive elements, two of candidate status, and **no element present in all five members**.

**Two structural claims, both confirmed, one needing re-siting.**

- **Nothing splits collateral along a state partition.** One element splits a claim along *time*; nothing splits one along *state*. This is the defining primitive of the entire sector, and its absence takes order matching with it: two of one venue's three settlement modes *are* that split and merge, so the missing primitive removes the matching engine too.
- **There is no element for a central counterparty** — but the shape is not the one expected. That venue now runs a clearing house **with no guaranty fund at all**, so the three symbols that were said to reconstruct a default waterfall reconstruct nothing there. Two further pieces, operator first-loss tranches and callable assessments, have no symbol either.

**A refutation to carry.** One allocator has a depositor-weighted veto, which refutes "no on-chain recourse". The real distinction is **preventive versus compensatory** recourse — the depositor can stop a change but cannot be made whole after one — and that is a sharper claim than the one it replaces.

**Also.** That venue's collateral is now a wrapped dollar that arms a peg-swap element the corpus record does not carry, and one allocator's product post-dates the corpus entirely.
