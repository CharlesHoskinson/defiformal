## Lane narrative

**What the section introduction should say.** How does a token stay at a dollar when what makes it worth a dollar is a bank account and a legal promise? This category carries the paper's sharpest claim: the footprint is ten elements, **none of them from the accounting, pool-pricing, execution, liquidity-catalysis, credit, solvency or risk-transfer groups**, so every symbol these issuers use is control-plane or attestation rather than mechanism. The vocabulary's resolution is inversely correlated with capital, and this is where that is starkest.

**The finding: the corpus's two identical-set claims are coding artefacts, not facts.** The guardian element is live in all five and coded in one; the permission gate is present in all five on the corpus's own reasoning for one of them; and the timelock element — one issuer runs a 24-hour timelock controller — is coded in none. Three further refutations: one issuer's reward distribution is on-chain, so the fee element is not a forced fit; one does real burn-and-mint cross-chain issuance, so cross-domain elements are missing from its record; and for one the cross-domain elements are unconfirmed. If those fall, three unlike systems share one signature — which is a finding about the vocabulary, not about the systems.

**The honest limit.** Round one could not name a single bank or custodian for any of the five, and could not retrieve two issuers' attestation figures. A second round is targeting exactly that, because the claim that everything determining the peg sits outside the model is only strong if the outside-the-model things are shown to be real and specific.
