# Requirements from Linear Logic, Session Types and Separation Logic

## 0. Position in one paragraph

Financial value is a resource: it may not be duplicated, and it may not be silently
discarded. Girard's linear logic (1987) is the logic of exactly that constraint, and
its absent structural rules — no contraction, no weakening — are not a restriction we
impose but the conservation law we are told is missing. The prior carrier is a subset
of a vocabulary; a subset lattice has contraction and weakening by definition, so it
*cannot* state a conservation law, and no amount of table-filling would have let it.
I require the carrier to change: a protocol is not a set of elements but a **typed
open process** — a term with named ports, each carrying a session type, over a linear
context of held resources and an unrestricted context of copyable information.
Composition is **cut**, not union. Conservation is then not an axiom to be added but a
theorem of the typing discipline (preservation), and non-freezing is a second theorem
(progress). Uniswap v4's `NonzeroDeltaCount == 0` is the runtime residue of a
statically decidable linear typing; deferred net settlement is a linear region whose
closing rule demands an empty delta context. That is the whole of the conservation
failure, resolved by a connective we already have.

## 1. Carrier and signature

**Ambient structure.** A **linear/non-linear (LNL) model** in Benton's sense (1994): a
symmetric monoidal adjunction `F ⊣ G : C → L` between a cartesian closed category `C`
(the *information plane* — copyable) and a symmetric monoidal closed category `L` (the
*value plane* — linear). `! = FG` is the exponential comonad. Protocols with multiple
ports live in the **coloured operad** (equivalently PROP) generated by `L`'s objects as
colours. State lives in a **unital resource algebra / camera** in the sense of Iris
(Jung–Krebbers–Birkedal et al., JFP 2018), i.e. a partial commutative monoid completed
with a validity predicate `✓` — the standard BI resource semantics of Pym–O'Hearn–Yang
(2004).

**Sorts** (colours of the operad; I claim these are the semantic content of G01–G16):

| Sort | Plane | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `Asset α` | linear | bearer units of denomination α |
| `Claim(π, φ)` | linear | entitlement against protocol `π` under condition `φ` |
| `Debt(A) := A^⊥` | linear | **liability is linear negation of a claim**; `A ⊗ A^⊥ ⊢ ⊥` is netting |
| `Pos = ⊗_i A_i ⊗ ⊗_j B_j^⊥` | linear | a position is a signed multiset; its *degree* is its conservation obligation |
| `Auth(r, op)` | linear or `!` | one-shot capability (signature, nonce, pause) vs. standing permission |
| `Obl^{≤d}(A)` | linear + modal | an obligation of type `A` maturing by deadline `d` |
| `!Price`, `!Attest(v,t)`, `!Param` | `!` | **genuinely copyable** |

**The two-plane discipline is the substance.** A price quote, an oracle reading, an
attestation, a public parameter, an address, a Merkle proof, the *fact* that a proof
exists — all are `!`-types: freely duplicable and discardable, and reading one twice is
sound. A claim on capital, a pool share, an LP position, a debt, a withdrawal-queue
ticket, an escrow receipt, an unlocked settlement scope, a one-shot authority — all are
strictly linear. The critical structural fact is a *missing rule*: dereliction
`!A ⊸ A` exists, but there is **no `A ⊸ !A`**. You cannot promote value to
information. That absence *is* the no-counterfeit law, stated once and inherited by
every construction.

**Typing judgement for a protocol.**

```
Γ ; Δ ⊢_ρ  P  ::  (x₁ : A₁ , … , xₙ : Aₙ)
```

`Γ` unrestricted (information), `Δ` linear (held value and capabilities), `ρ` a grade
drawn from an ordered semiring `R` (below), `P` the protocol term, and the right-hand
side its **ports** with session types. Multi-party protocols carry a **global type** `G`
in the sense of Honda–Yoshida–Carbone (2008), with role projection `G ↾ r` giving each
party its local type; roles are the missing *party sort*, and indexed roles `r : Role[n]`
(Deniélou–Yoshida 2011) give quorums and delegated agents their arity.

**Elements are of two kinds, and the old vocabulary conflated them.** `Sh`, `Cp`, `Cd`,
`Op`, `Tr` are **type constructors**. `Ct` (collateral-threshold test), `Li`, `Ad`, `Sl`
are **typing rules** — `Ct` is the guard on a `⊸`-elimination, and `Li` is its
failure-branch eliminator. This explains, without fitting, the recorded fact that `Ct`
is a consequence in every protocol that has it and a primitive in none: a rule is not a
member of a set. It also explains why the definite requirement digraph `D` is thin at
15 arcs on 58 vertices — `D` is an under-sampled shadow of the subterm/elimination
relation, visible only where both endpoints happened to be nameable as constructors.

**Grading.** `!_ρ A` over a semiring `R` — Girard–Scedrov–Scott's bounded linear logic
(1992), in the modern semiring form of Brunel–Gaboardi–Mazza–Zdancewic (2014),
Ghica–Smith (2014) and Atkey's Quantitative Type Theory (2018). Taking
`R = (ℝ≥0 ∪ {∞}, +, ×, ≤)` makes magnitudes first-class: `!_{1.5} Collateral`, and
"manipulation cost < position value" becomes the grade inequality `ρ_manip < ρ_pos`,
a machine-checkable side condition rather than one of the 9 prose prohibition rows.

**Stratum 0–4 is a filtration, not a semiring grade.** Composition does not add strata,
so it is not a degree function on ⊗. I require it be read as
`λ^{(0)} ⊆ λ^{(1)} ⊆ … ⊆ λ^{(4)}`, with `stratum(t) = 1 + max{stratum(s) : s a subterm}`
— i.e. **derivation depth in the ⊸-elimination order**. G01–G16 are the colours;
strata are the filtration degree. `PO-TYPE-7` tests this.

## 2. Composition

Two composition operators, both closed by construction.

**Interface layer: cut.** For `P :: (x : A)` and `Q` with `x : A^⊥` in its context,
`(νx)(P | Q)` is the cut. This is a *rule of the calculus*: applying it to two
well-typed terms yields a well-typed term, by the statement of the rule. Closure is
therefore definitional, never measured. Cut is **partial**, and its side condition is
exactly **duality** `B = A^⊥`, decidable in time linear in the size of the type
(coinductively for recursive session types, by standard automaton bisimulation). A
partial composition with matching domains and codomains is not a defect — it is what a
category *is*, and the multi-port version is a coloured operad. Both are total once you
say what the objects are.

**State layer: separating conjunction.** `P * Q`, defined when the footprints are
disjoint. `(*, emp)` is a partial commutative monoid; completing it to a resource
algebra with a validity predicate `✓` makes the operation **total on the carrier**, with
admissibility recovered as `✓(a · b)` — a property of the algebra rather than a
measurement over a corpus. O'Hearn's **frame rule**,
`{P} C {Q} ⟹ {P * R} C {Q * R}`, is the composition-preservation theorem in this
idiom: a protocol's specification survives being placed beside an untouched
environment, *provided footprints are disjoint*. The prior work's composition failure is
precisely a failure of framing, and disjointness is the thing elements-as-sets cannot
express and `*` expresses natively.

**What well-typed composition guarantees.** (i) **Conservation**: no rule of `Δ`
duplicates or drops a linear hypothesis, so the multiset of asset atoms is invariant
across every step of every composite. (ii) **Session fidelity**: each port is used
according to its declared protocol. (iii) **Deadlock freedom**: cut elimination —
Caires–Pfenning (2010), Wadler's *Propositions as Sessions* (2014) — no composite has a
cyclic wait. (iv) **No leaked value**: the empty-context closure rule.

## 3. Completeness theorem (formal statement)

Completeness in my idiom is **expressive adequacy of a core calculus**, proved by
translation plus soundness — not a clone-theoretic classification. Fix

`λ_DeFi` := ILL with `⊗, ⊸, 1`; additives `&, ⊕, 0, ⊤`; graded `!_ρ` over `R`;
recursive session types `μ, ν` (Toninho–Caires–Pfenning 2014); a deadline modality
`◇_{≤d}` (Bocchi–Yang–Yoshida, *Timed Multiparty Session Types*, 2014); and role
quantification for MPST.

> **Theorem (Adequacy).** For each application `A` in the corpus of 60 there is a
> `λ_DeFi` term `⟦A⟧` and a typing `Γ_A ; Δ_A ⊢_{ρ_A} ⟦A⟧ :: G_A` such that
> **(i)** `⟦A⟧` is well-typed; **(ii)** the labelled transition system of `A`'s deployed
> interface is weakly bisimilar to that of `⟦A⟧`; **(iii)** every recorded obligation of
> `A` is discharged in `⟦A⟧` either by a term of the obligation type **or** by a vacuity
> certificate (§below), with no residue.

**Falsification.** Adequacy fails if some corpus application's LTS is weakly bisimilar
to no `λ_DeFi` term at any type — concretely, if a mechanism demands a rule the calculus
lacks.

**The analogue of Post's maximal clones.** In a typed calculus, the ways a candidate
core can fail to be complete are the **closure properties it cannot escape**. A calculus
is inadequate iff every corpus mechanism lies inside one of these closed classes:

1. **Duplication-free** — needs `A ⊸ A ⊗ A`. *Escape:* `Rb` (rebasing) looks like
   duplication and is not; it is a change of unit, typed as
   `Share ⊗ !Ix ≅ Share' ⊗ !Ix'` with the index copyable. Witness required.
2. **Sink-free** — needs weakening. *Escape:* burn, slash and socialised loss are not
   weakening; they are communication to a designated sink role, `burn : A ⊸ 1` at role
   `⊥`. The calculus must be linear-with-explicit-sinks, **not affine**.
3. **Deterministic** — needs adversarial external choice (ordering, MEV). *Escape:*
   `⊕` plus a scheduler role; if genuine nondeterminism is required, Atkey–Lindley–
   Morris (2016) / Kokke–Morris–Wadler HCP.
4. **Ungraded** — needs thresholds. *Escape:* `!_ρ` over ordered `R`.
5. **First-order** — needs protocols as arguments. *Escape:* `⊸` is higher-order, so a
   curator is literally `(V₁ & … & V_n) ⊸ Claim`. This is the single largest
   expressiveness gain over a subset lattice and it is free in a λ-calculus.
6. **Non-recursive / non-self-referential** — needs upgradeable proxies. *Escape:*
   `μ`-types plus a governance rewrite modality (`Up`, `Tg`).

**Completeness check.** For each of the six, exhibit a corpus application that *requires*
escaping it. Six witnesses plus a total translation is the proof obligation set; that is
what "functionally complete over this subject matter" means here.

**Progress and preservation, and what they buy operationally.**

- **Preservation** (subject reduction, in Wright–Felleisen's syntactic style, 1994): if
  `Δ ⊢ P :: G` and `P → P'` then `Δ' ⊢ P' :: G'` with the linear measure unchanged.
  Operationally: **no transition — swap, borrow, liquidate, bridge, upgrade — creates or
  destroys value, and no interface obligation silently mutates.** Solvency and supply
  conservation are typings, and preservation says they survive every step. This is the
  "no theft, no inflation" half of the security surface.
- **Progress**: a well-typed closed process is a value or steps; in the session setting,
  it neither deadlocks nor gets stuck holding an undischarged obligation.
  Operationally: **no locked funds.** Every escrow, withdrawal queue, unlock scope and
  in-flight cross-domain message has a continuation that can fire.

Preservation = no theft or inflation. Progress = no freeze. Two theorems cover the
observed loss surface.

## 4. Construction / synthesis

**Synthesis problem.** Given a specification `S` — a global session type `G` together
with a linear resource pre/post pair `(Δ_in, Δ_out)` and grade bounds — find `P` with
`Γ ; Δ_in ⊢_ρ P :: G` and `Δ_out` at closure. This is **proof search in ILL**, and
proof search in full ILL is undecidable (Lincoln–Mitchell–Scedrov–Shankar, 1992), so
decidability must be bought by restriction, and I require it be bought at the multiplicative-
additive fragment: **MALL proof search is PSPACE-complete** (Lincoln et al.), and the
**Horn fragment of linear logic** — which is what the corpus's element vocabulary
actually looks like — is decidable, with the propositional Horn-LL reachability problem
equivalent to Petri-net reachability (Kanovich, 1995). That equivalence is the working
tool: a protocol's asset flow *is* a Petri net, and proof-net construction *is* the
place-transition matching.

**Certificate.** The certificate that a construction discharges a specification is the
**proof net** itself, checkable in linear time by Danos–Regnier's correctness criterion
(1989): a cut-free, correct net is a machine-verifiable witness that every port is
matched exactly once and nothing leaks. This replaces "name an element" with "exhibit a
term", and a term is checkable.

**Formalization discipline** (the refusal to count prose): *a constraint counts only if
it is a typing rule* — premises are judgements over named types, conclusion is a
judgement, and there is a term former. A row with an `[ext]` term is not an incomplete
rule; it is not a rule. Mechanization target: Iris in Rocq (linearity, ghost state,
invariants, and the frame rule already present) or a graded embedding in Agda.

## 5. Disposition of the prior obstructions

- **"Admissibility not preserved under composition" (Uniswap ∪ Aave arms X2).**
  **Dissolves.** Union is not a composition operator on ported objects; it forgets which
  scope each element inhabits. Cut does not forget. `X21` — flash liquidity `Fl`
  co-present with `Xf`/`Rl`/`Of`, credited with 147 of 185 failures — is a **scope
  confusion**: `Fl` is a linearity constraint *inside one atomic settlement region*
  (`borrow ⊸ repay` within a single unlock scope), while `Xf`/`Of` inhabit a region
  spanning a challenge window. The paper's own Uniswap entry says the two "never meet"
  and that an element set has no way to say so. A linear region says it in the type. The
  prohibition survives only as a side condition *on cut within a shared region*, where it
  is checked by the same decidable duality test — so admissibility becomes preserved by
  the rule rather than measured after the fact.
- **"Horn classes are not union-closed."** **Not applicable.** There is no subset
  lattice. The residual, honest obstruction is that cut is partial; its exact side
  condition is duality, linear-time decidable, and partiality of composition is the
  definition of a category rather than a defect.
- **"Kripke–Kleene fixpoint collapses to `(⊥,⊤)`."** **Converted to a computable
  invariant.** There is no powerset to iterate over. The corresponding fixpoint is
  **cut-elimination normal form**, which carries maximal rather than zero information: it
  *is* the protocol's normal behaviour, and it is computable.
- **"The diagonal of admissibility is unreachable from the componentwise product."**
  **Localized and reinterpreted.** `Δ(P) = P ⊗ P` does not typecheck for linear `P`, and
  that is *correct*: a protocol holding capital cannot be duplicated. The obstruction was
  a true fact reported in a language that could not distinguish it from a defect.
- **"The positive theory excludes nothing (0.0% median exclusion)."** **Converted to a
  measurable.** In a type system, exclusion rate is type-error rate; see §7.

## The "obligation cannot arise" failure, directly

The requirement language admits exactly one way to discharge a term: name an element.
That is an **introduction rule with no left rule**. A sequent calculus has both. In
`λ_DeFi` an obligation `O` with trigger type `T` is discharged in **two** first-class
ways:

1. **Witness discharge**: a term `t : O`.
2. **Vacuity discharge**: a term `v : T ⊸ 0` — a proof that the trigger is
   *uninhabited*, followed by `absurd : 0 ⊸ O`.

For an options venue escrowing the maximum payoff at trade time, the margin-call trigger
is `Shortfall := Payoff ⊗ Escrow^⊥`. Under the escrow invariant `!_{ρ_E} Escrow` with
`ρ_E ≥ max ρ_Payoff` — a grade inequality, checkable — `Shortfall` is uninhabited, and
`void : Shortfall ⊸ 0` is an ordinary program in an ordinary type. `Ct` and `Li` are
then discharged, not open. This is first-class rather than a special case because in
linear logic `A^⊥ = A ⊸ 0` *is a type*: refutation and construction are the same kind of
object, and a vacuity certificate is checked by the same typechecker as a witness. The
paper's own judgement — "the repair is to the constraint language rather than to the
vocabulary" — is exactly the addition of the left rule.

## 6. Minimal viable enrichment (ranked by payoff / cost)

1. **Ports and linear scopes.** Assign each of the 58 elements an input/output colour
   signature over G01–G16, and mark each occurrence with its **atomic region**
   (transaction / epoch / challenge window). Cost: a 58-row table, days. Payoff:
   composition becomes cut; closure holds by construction; ≥79% of recorded composition
   failures (`X21`) are testable as scope artifacts; interface automata and
   assume-guarantee contract algebra (de Alfaro–Henzinger; Benveniste et al.) transfer
   wholesale, because their missing precondition was ported objects. **Take this one.**
2. **Linear negation as liability** (`Debt(A) = A^⊥`). Cost: one connective. Payoff:
   netting, conservation and solvency become typings; Uniswap v4 deferred settlement is
   expressible. Rides on (1) at <10% extra cost.
3. **Vacuity certificates** (`T ⊸ 0`). Cost: one discharge rule plus a proof format.
   Payoff: immediate reclassification of the escrowed-payoff venues and a measurable
   slice of the 689 undischarged obligations.
4. **Semiring grading `!_ρ`** (magnitudes). Converts 9 prose prohibition rows to clauses.
5. **Roles / MPST** (party sort). Separates USDT from USD1.
6. **Higher-order protocols-as-arguments** (curators, aggregators-of-aggregators).
   Requires 1 and 2 first; then free.

## 7. Falsifiable near-term test

**Test T0 — the scope test on X21.** *Input:* the 147 protocol pairs recorded as arming
`X21`, together with each pair's decomposition and primary sources. *Procedure:* for each
pair, classify the `Fl` occurrence and the `Xf`/`Rl`/`Of` occurrence into atomic regions
— same transaction, same epoch, or disjoint regions — from the recorded call paths.
*Verdict:* if **≥ 90%** of the 147 place the two occurrences in **disjoint regions**,
then `X21` is a scope-confusion artifact of an unported carrier, region-annotation alone
eliminates ≥ 79% of all recorded composition failures, and the requirement in §6.1 is
confirmed. If the fraction is below 60%, the port hypothesis is refuted for this corpus
and the co-presence prohibition is genuinely global. Days, not months; the paper's own
Uniswap entry is one already-classified data point on the confirming side.

**Test T1 — the exclusion repair.** *Input:* the 72 real protocols and 84 synthetic
corruptions already generated. *Procedure:* build the port-signature table from §6.1,
compile each subject to a Petri-net / proof-net skeleton, and run Danos–Regnier
correctness. *Verdict:* the checker must **accept a majority of the 72 real protocols and
reject a majority of the 84 corruptions**. Any positive correlation refutes the prior
finding that the repaired operator is anti-correlated with reality; a non-positive
correlation refutes my port signature and sends it back to the table, not to the theory.

## 8. Named proof obligations

- **PO-TYPE-1** (*Adequacy*). There is a compositional `⟦−⟧` from the 60 corpus
  applications into `λ_DeFi` with each `⟦A⟧` well-typed and weakly bisimilar to `A`'s
  deployed interface LTS.
- **PO-TYPE-2** (*Conservation = preservation*). If `Γ;Δ ⊢_ρ P :: G` and `P → P'`, then
  `Γ;Δ' ⊢_{ρ'} P' :: G'` with the asset-atom multiset of `Δ` and `Δ'` equal.
- **PO-TYPE-3** (*Progress / no locked funds*). Every well-typed closed `P` is a value or
  steps; no reachable `P'` holds an undischarged `Obl^{≤d}` past `d`.
- **PO-TYPE-4** (*Uniswap v4*). The v4 unlock scope is typed `Δ ⊢ body :: 1`, and
  `NonzeroDeltaCount == 0` is derivable from that typing — the runtime check is redundant
  under the static discipline.
- **PO-TYPE-5** (*Vacuity discharge*). For a venue escrowing max payoff, the grade
  inequality `ρ_Escrow ≥ max ρ_Payoff` yields a closed term `Shortfall ⊸ 0`, and every
  obligation of `Ct`, `Li`, `Bs` is discharged by `absurd` composed with it.
- **PO-TYPE-6** (*X21 is a scope condition*). `X21` is derivable as a side condition on
  cut restricted to a shared atomic region, and is vacuous across disjoint regions.
- **PO-TYPE-7** (*Stratum is a filtration*). For every corpus term,
  `stratum(t) = 1 + max{stratum(s) : s ⊑ t}` up to the recorded 0–4 labels; equivalently,
  `λ^{(n)}` is generated from `λ^{(n-1)}` by one connective class.
- **PO-TYPE-8** (*G01–G16 are colours*). The 16 groups are a sound colouring of the
  operad: every element's port signature has inputs and outputs typed within the group
  assignment, with no cross-group port left uncoloured.
- **PO-TYPE-9** (*Rules vs constructors*). `Ct, Li, Ad, Sl, Sv` are typing rules, not type
  constructors; re-sorting them makes `D` a genuine elimination order with strictly more
  than 15 arcs.
- **PO-TYPE-10** (*Decidable synthesis*). Restricted to the Horn fragment, the synthesis
  problem is decidable and equivalent to Petri-net reachability (Kanovich); the corpus's
  instances lie in the fragment.
- **PO-TYPE-11** (*Frame rule transfers*). Protocol specifications are frameable:
  `{P} π {Q}` implies `{P * R} π {Q * R}` for every `R` with footprint disjoint from
  `π`'s; disjointness is computed from the port table of §6.1.
- **PO-TYPE-12** (*No promotion*). No corpus mechanism requires `A ⊸ !A` for `A` an asset
  or claim sort. A counterexample is a genuine counterfeit and must be reported as one.

**Conjecture (Region Separation).** Every co-presence prohibition recorded in the corpus
is either (a) vacuous once occurrences are annotated with atomic regions, or (b) derivable
as a duality failure on cut within a single region. No corpus prohibition is irreducibly
global. Test T0 is the first instance.
