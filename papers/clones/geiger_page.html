Closed systems of functions and predicates

David Scott Geiger
Vol. 27 (1968), No. 1, 95–100
DOI: 10.2140/pjm.1968.27.95
Abstract

In this paper we show that there is a one to one correspondence between systems of functions defined on a finite set A and systems of predicates defined on A. This result implies that a complete set of invariants for a universal algebra on A is given by predicates defined on A. Conversely functions on A provide a complete system of invariants for sets of predicates closed under conjunction, change of variable and application of the existential quantifier.
