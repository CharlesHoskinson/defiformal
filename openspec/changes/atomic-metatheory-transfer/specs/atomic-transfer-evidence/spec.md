## Purpose

Binds the proposed Atomic metatheory to independently expected financial executions, real mutations, actual proof inventories and accepted dependency gates.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: FE01 Independent financial and field coverage

Evidence SHALL execute all20 planned fixture families with independent full speculative/public expectations and distinguish synthetic observer pairs from real executions.

#### Scenario: E01 The three-stream loan plus two funded peer family runs

- **WHEN** the three-stream loan plus two funded peer family runs
- **THEN** all12 complete schedules and actual tree shapes preserve the literal old transient observations.

#### Scenario: E02 A preserved public or diagnostic field is changed alone

- **WHEN** a preserved public or diagnostic field is changed alone
- **THEN** the production comparator detects it, including full off-policy outstanding entries in synthetic diagnostic pairs.

#### Scenario: E03 Candidate execution or projection manufactures expected values

- **WHEN** candidate execution or projection manufactures expected values
- **THEN** the result does not qualify as an independent financial oracle.

### Requirement: FE02 Real mutations and controls

Evidence SHALL execute the18 specified runtime mutants and all accepted predecessor controls with complete inventories, protected positives and honest blocked classification.

#### Scenario: E04 A runtime mutant compiles and changes the designated semantics

- **WHEN** a runtime mutant compiles and changes the designated semantics
- **THEN** its intended comparison fails while all checks execute and protected positives pass.

#### Scenario: E05 A compiler error, timeout, empty inventory or wrong Audit protocol occurs

- **WHEN** a compiler error, timeout, empty inventory or wrong Audit protocol occurs
- **THEN** the attempt is blocked, preserved and not counted as detection.

#### Scenario: E06 Predecessor tests are adapted

- **WHEN** predecessor tests are adapted
- **THEN** the exact complete source/argument/error/proof-marker mapping and all actual inherited control identities are recorded.

### Requirement: FE03 Accepted API refresh before planning freeze

Official freeze SHALL require accepted S10/M3/M4/M5 and current Atomic source/semantics/runner refresh; provisional names SHALL not be treated as implemented declarations.

#### Scenario: E07 Accepted predecessor APIs are unavailable or changed

- **WHEN** accepted predecessor APIs are unavailable or changed
- **THEN** freeze remains blocked until explicit refresh/revision and fresh scoped baselines.

#### Scenario: E08 Same-candidate planning reviews have not both passed

- **WHEN** same-candidate planning reviews have not both passed
- **THEN** implementation remains unauthorized regardless of OpenSpec artifact completeness.

### Requirement: FE04 Full proof and final evidence acceptance

Acceptance SHALL bind actual proof types/premises/axioms and every scenario to executed evidence plus required independent reviews and scoped delivery.

#### Scenario: E09 The proof inventory is produced

- **WHEN** the proof inventory is produced
- **THEN** generic, concrete, counterexample and generated declarations are distinguished with no sorry/custom axiom/native_decide in accepted new proofs.

#### Scenario: E10 Required native results and all scoped checks pass

- **WHEN** required native results and all scoped checks pass
- **THEN** the exact candidate is delivered/read back/archived with dynamic provenance, new-lane interactions, boundary reassociation and fidelity limits still explicit.
