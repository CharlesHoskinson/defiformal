The Elements of DeFi
DeFi does not have an innovation problem. It has a vocabulary problem.
Nearly every protocol ever shipped is a recombination of about fifty
recurring mechanisms — and once you can name them, most of the
industry stops looking novel and starts looking legible.

  Call it a periodic table if you like, but it is not one: no periodic law, no
  atomic number, nothing recurring down a column. Chemistry discovered
  its elements. These were designed, in a hurry, by people shipping to
  production — which is exactly why the combinations are the part worth
  writing down.
