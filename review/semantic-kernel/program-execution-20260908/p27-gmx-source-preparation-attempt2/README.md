The collateral-sufficiency check instead deducts negative realized PnL and does not credit positive PnL. PnL may therefore affect liquidation health without becoming spendable collateral tokens. The later PnL-as-cash mutant must target the correct observation; removing PnL from the liquidation equation would not represent source fidelity.

Claimables are stored by market/token/account and transferred by a separate claim path. A signed net equation must preserve token identities, conversion prices, rounding, fee liabilities and claimable assets. It must not relabel all of them as one cash field.
