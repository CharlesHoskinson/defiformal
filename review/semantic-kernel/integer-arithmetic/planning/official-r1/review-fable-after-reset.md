Q07 lists F40 and F41 under "recipient and collector are the same cell", but they are payer coincidences. Either amend the scenario text or split them into their own scenarios at task 5.3.

6. **Monotonicity wording differs between design section 2 and P08.** Section 2 says mulDiv is monotone "in the amount"; P08 says "in either operand". Both are true for positive denominators. Pick one statement so the exported theorem is checked against a single contract.
7. **Unreachable error tags.** The `subUnderflow` tag passed to `Word.checked` inside `sub` and `feeFromGross` can never fire, because a difference is below the minuend's bound.
