Submit Frequently Asked Questions What is Rysk Premium? + Rysk Premium is onchain vault infrastructure for volatility income. It provides the vault contracts, RFQ liquidity, onchain execution, collateralization, NAV accounting, and settlement needed to launch options income products. The curator defines and runs the strategy; depositors earn volatility income without running the strategy themselves. What strategies and vault products can be built? + Covered calls and cash-secured puts are live today, with vertical spreads coming soon. These can be packaged into income vaults, accumulation vaults, ecosystem vaults, structured vaults, and institutional mandates, on effectively any onchain asset with pricing and liquidity, including majors like BTC and ETH, HYPE, and staking or yield-bearing assets. How does a vault work? + Depositors add funds to a segregated vault contract; the curator never receives or custodies them. The curator selects which options to write and at what strikes and expiries; orders are priced through the RFQ network or agreed OTC and execute onchain. Vaults run in epochs: options trade freely within an epoch while deposits and withdrawals queue, then execute at the struck NAV at each epoch boundary so everyone enters and exits at fair value. How is collateral secured? + Positions are fully collateralized onchain. Once a trade is filled, the backing collateral is locked in Rysk’s settlement layer for the life of the trade and never leaves the contract, with no rehypothecation, no shared portfolio risk, and no unsecured bilateral counterparty credit risk. What is the RFQ network? + A single onchain venue that aggregates signed quotes from integrated market makers. A curator requests quotes across multiple strikes and expiries at once, compares them side by side, and executes the best onchain. Short positions can be bought back early through the same flow. 5+ market makers are integrated and trading today, with more institutional desks onboarding. Can vaults be permissioned for institutions? + Yes. Permissioned vaults gate deposits by KYC and whitelist and can restrict quoting to a whitelist of approved market makers, ideal for institutional mandates with compliance requirements. Permissionless vaults are open to any onchain depositor on the same RFQ network and settlement. Is there a live institutional vault? + Yes. Hyperion DeFi (NASDAQ: HYPD), the first U.S. publicly-listed company building a treasury around Hyperliquid’s HYPE, runs a live, permissioned volatility income vault on Rysk Premium. It writes cash-secured puts in USDC to earn income on treasury cash while setting target prices to accumulate more HYPE. Is Rysk Premium audited and battle-tested? + Rysk Premium runs on Rysk V12, live on HyperEVM mainnet since June 2025, and is built on the Opyn / Gamma options protocol for issuance, collateralization, and settlement. It has completed 5 audits by Trust Security. Reports are at docs.rysk.finance/security .
