## Why

The corpus preserves 72 historical rows as 75 development candidates, but all
candidate deployments remain unresolved. Its 29 annotation differences, separate
Liquity V1 source challenge and missing original reference mappings need explicit,
versioned decisions before stronger coverage or fidelity claims are possible.

## What Changes

- Add immutable primary-source captures and honest original-reference recovery or
  replacement records, retaining historical rows, citation tokens and attachments
  as unresolved unless actual provenance is recovered.
- Add organization/product/version/deployment resolution, auditable additional
  product splits, source pins, dependency edges and ambiguity/residue records.
- Add reusable, versioned evidence rules and an append-only adjudication overlay
  for all 29 differences and the separate agreed-label Liquity V1 challenge.
- Add distinct source-code, deployment and implementation-to-model fidelity
  statuses; unknown evidence is never promoted by agreement or a source URL.
- Freeze separate development and reserved-evaluation manifests with exposure
  controls. All existing 75 candidates and their design-used derivatives remain
  development; this change does not acquire or evaluate new holdout semantics.
- Add deterministic offline build/check tooling, actual CLI failure controls,
  source-bound reports and independent native review before acceptance.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `corpus-source-provenance`: Captured primary evidence, historical scope and
  original citation/attachment recovery versus reconstructed replacement support.
- `corpus-identity-dependencies`: Candidate identity resolution, traceable splits,
  typed dependencies, explicit residue, source/deployment pins and fidelity limits.
- `corpus-evidence-adjudication`: Reusable claim rules, immutable annotation
  observations, complete disagreement/challenge inventory and reviewed overlays.
- `corpus-evaluation-boundary`: Permanent development exposure, freeze records and
  a separate reserved-evaluation manifest with honest empty/blocked status.
- `corpus-adjudication-evidence`: Deterministic actual CLI verification,
  discriminating controls, complete inventories and exact source/review bindings.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Accepted historical normalization and existing kernel semantics retain
their behavior and artifacts.
