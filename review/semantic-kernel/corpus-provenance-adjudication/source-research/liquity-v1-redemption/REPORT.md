The proposed rule requires a holder-submitted claim/token to settle against backing or payout under stated conditions. A secondary-market sale or borrower repayment alone is insufficient. The version-specific FAQ describes LUSD exchanged for ETH, including a redemption fee, and explicitly distinguishes this from paying back one’s own debt. Its V1 page title and URL supply version-specific attribution. [Official V1 redemption FAQ](https://docs.liquity.org/liquity-v1/faq/lusd-redemptions).

The retained ETH/LUSD contracts are pinned to **`liquity/dev` commit `3e64ee1b52c50d51587c64c1cf75e0ba82934979`**. `redeemCollateral` accepts a caller’s requested LUSD amount; the per-Trove helper calculates corresponding ETH and reduces selected Trove debt/collateral. Final settlement calls the token burn for the amount actually redeemed, decreases ActivePool debt, and pays the caller ETH after fees. Full redemption closes the Trove and assigns its remaining collateral to the borrower through CollSurplusPool. This directly supports the rule’s settlement criterion. [Pinned redemption route and helpers](https://github.com/liquity/dev/blob/3e64ee1b52c50d51587c64c1cf75e0ba82934979/packages/contracts/contracts/TroveManager.sol#L816-L1023).

The source qualifies any unconditional interpretation: bootstrap timing, system collateral ratio, positive amount, sufficient caller balance and fee checks apply. Iteration limits or a cancelled partial redemption can leave part of the request unfilled. Outdated partial hints or insufficient remaining net debt cancel that partial step; zero ETH drawn refuses execution. Full closure also checks that more than one Trove remains. These are source conditions, not tested execution guarantees. [Pinned guard helpers](https://github.com/liquity/dev/blob/3e64ee1b52c50d51587c64c1cf75e0ba82934979/packages/contracts/contracts/TroveManager.sol#L1484-L1508).

`LiquityBase._requireUserAcceptsFee` checks the computed fee fraction against the caller’s maximum; the shared base also defines the minimum collateral ratio. Thus the FAQ’s “at any time without limitations” wording should not be promoted to a universal contract claim. [Pinned fee guard](https://github.com/liquity/dev/blob/3e64ee1b52c50d51587c64c1cf75e0ba82934979/packages/contracts/contracts/Dependencies/LiquityBase.sol#L82-L85).

Borrower repayment is a different operation: `repayLUSD` adjusts the caller’s own Trove, while `_repayLUSD` burns the paying account’s LUSD and reduces debt. The redemption decision therefore does not follow merely from evidence that borrowers can repay. [Pinned borrower-repayment entry](https://github.com/liquity/dev/blob/3e64ee1b52c50d51587c64c1cf75e0ba82934979/packages/contracts/contracts/BorrowerOperations.sol#L233-L236), [repayment helper](https://github.com/liquity/dev/blob/3e64ee1b52c50d51587c64c1cf75e0ba82934979/packages/contracts/contracts/BorrowerOperations.sol#L458-L463).
