| Path | Source inspection |
|---|---|
| Normal liquidation | The batch route checks ICR against MCR, then closes selected Troves as `closedByLiquidation`. It computes an offset portion and redistributes remaining debt/collateral. |
| Recovery liquidation | Conditions select pure redistribution, mixed handling, or capped full offset with surplus assigned to the borrower. Some branches do nothing; the last-Trove constraint also matters. |
| Redistribution | Debt moves from ActivePool to DefaultPool alongside collateral; reward accumulators allocate obligations to surviving Troves. Debt redistribution is not debt repayment or cancellation. |

These distinctions are visible in the pinned [TroveManager](https://github.com/liquity/dev/blob/3e64ee1b52c50d51587c64c1cf75e0ba82934979/packages/contracts/contracts/TroveManager.sol#L303-L480), including its [redistribution accounting](https://github.com/liquity/dev/blob/3e64ee1b52c50d51587c64c1cf75e0ba82934979/packages/contracts/contracts/TroveManager.sol#L1203-L1237). Exact retained-byte locators are in `evidence-locators.json`.

**Offsetting** burns Stability Pool LUSD, reduces recorded liquidated debt and transfers collateral into the pool. In this specific pin, `getMaxAmountToOffset` reserves `MIN_LUSD_IN_SP` and can further reduce the available budget. Thus “offset capacity” is not interchangeable with the full deposit total. Gas compensation and capped-branch surplus also prevent an unconditional entire-collateral-to-pool statement. This source behavior is not asserted for any deployed address. [Pinned StabilityPool](https://github.com/liquity/dev/blob/3e64ee1b52c50d51587c64c1cf75e0ba82934979/packages/contracts/contracts/StabilityPool.sol#L495-L527), [offset accounting](https://github.com/liquity/dev/blob/3e64ee1b52c50d51587c64c1cf75e0ba82934979/packages/contracts/contracts/StabilityPool.sol#L628-L645).

**Voluntary repayment** follows `repayLUSD` into a borrower-authorized adjustment with balance, amount and collateral-ratio checks. `_repayLUSD` burns that account’s tokens and reduces pool debt. Full owner closure is a separate guarded call that repays borrower debt and the gas reserve before returning collateral. Merely observing token burning therefore does not identify liquidation. [Pinned BorrowerOperations](https://github.com/liquity/dev/blob/3e64ee1b52c50d51587c64c1cf75e0ba82934979/packages/contracts/contracts/BorrowerOperations.sol#L233-L236), [owner closure](https://github.com/liquity/dev/blob/3e64ee1b52c50d51587c64c1cf75e0ba82934979/packages/contracts/contracts/BorrowerOperations.sol#L321-L356), [repayment helper](https://github.com/liquity/dev/blob/3e64ee1b52c50d51587c64c1cf75e0ba82934979/packages/contracts/contracts/BorrowerOperations.sol#L458-L463).
