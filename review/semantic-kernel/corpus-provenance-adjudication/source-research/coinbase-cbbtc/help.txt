Coinbase wrapped assets
Coinbase allows eligible customers to send wrapped versions of certain cryptocurrencies to wallet addresses on supported blockchains, enabling customers to transact on multiple networks while maintaining a single, unified balance of the native tokens in their Coinbase accounts. Each wrapped asset is backed 1:1 by its original cryptocurrency.
When transferring certain tokens via alternative networks, the sent funds may be converted into wrapped tokens. Always confirm the recipient supports the network you’re using before starting the transfer.

Send wrapped assets
To send a Coinbase wrapped asset:
Sign in to your Coinbase account.
Select
Transfer
, then
Send crypto
.
Enter the recipient's address.
Choose the cryptocurrency you want to send (BTC for cbBTC, DOGE for cbDOGE, XRP for cbXRP, ADA for cbADA, LTC for cbLTC, MEGA for cbMEGA
,
HYPE for cbHYPE, or ZEC for cbZEC
).
Select the desired network.
Enter the amount you want to send.
Review the transaction details, then select
Send now
.
Receive wrapped assets
To receive a Coinbase wrapped asset:
Sign in to your Coinbase account.
Select
Transfer
, then
Receive crypto.
Choose the cryptocurrency you want to receive (BTC for cbBTC, DOGE for cbDOGE, XRP for cbXRP, ADA for cbADA, LTC for cbLTC, MEGA for cbMEGA
,
HYPE for cbHYPE, or ZEC for cbZEC
).
Select the appropriate network.
Share your address by copying it, or allow the sender to scan your QR code.
Note: When you receive a Coinbase wrapped asset, Coinbase automatically unwraps it and credits your account with the underlying asset. For example, sending cbBTC to your Coinbase account will increase your BTC balance.
