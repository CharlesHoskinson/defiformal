Frequently asked questions
What is a stablecoin?
Stablecoins are a type of cryptocurrency designed to have a steady value over time relative to a reference asset, for example, the U.S. dollar. They can provide inclusive, broad access to the financial system, and can enable fast and efficient money movement. Stablecoins are programmable, offering developers a useful digital currency that can be built into public blockchains and can help link the traditional economy and Web3.
PayPal USD is a stablecoin that’s backed by US dollar deposits, US treasuries, and similar cash equivalents. You can buy and sell 1 PYUSD for 1 USD on PayPal.

How do PYUSD rewards work?
PayPal lets you earn rewards for holding PYUSD in your PayPal account. Rewards are calculated using your average daily balance of PYUSD and are paid out monthly in PYUSD. The reward rate is variable. You can view pending rewards within your Crypto hub.
To participate, you must:
Be signed up for crypto
Opt in to earn rewards
Hold a minimum balance of 1 PYUSD
You can also opt out at any time. If you do so, you'll stop accruing new rewards immediately, and any rewards already earned will be paid out on the next scheduled payment date.
